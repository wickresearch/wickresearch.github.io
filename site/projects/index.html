Analytical work

Tools built to support the research process. Each one is live on this site and documented in the public repository.

Wick macro terminal

A compact macro workspace covering rates and curves (US, Germany, UK, Japan, Canada), the Fed's front end, central-bank policy, US liquidity, inflation and labour, cross-asset analytics, and risk. All data is end-of-day or latest-published from public sources, and each series shows its provider, data type and timestamps.

Verified data pipeline

A Python pipeline run on a schedule by GitHub Actions. It draws on nine official providers (FRED, NY Fed, U.S. Treasury and FiscalData, Bundesbank, Bank of England, Bank of Canada, MoF Japan, BIS) plus Coinbase and handles retries, validation, incremental updates and last-known-good fallback. Every source was tested before it was used, and the record is published.

Rates risk and position sizing

DV01 by curve bucket for Treasury and STIR-futures positions, with parallel, steepening, flattening and policy-repricing shocks and replays of historical curve moves. Position sizing is computed from volatility, stop distance, DV01 budget and margin, with correlation to the existing book.

Risk module →

Portfolio risk and stress testing

For a user-defined multi-asset portfolio: volatility, drawdown, Sharpe and Sortino ratios, historical VaR and expected shortfall, and component risk contributions. Includes historical episode replays and factor-shock scenarios that show their betas and assumptions.

Event studies

How yields, the dollar, equities and commodities moved around FOMC decisions, CPI and payrolls releases, and policy-rate changes. Shows the mean and interquartile path and the per-event table. Event dates come from official calendars and FRED release dates.

Growth and inflation regimes

A deliberately simple, publication-lagged classification by the direction of growth and inflation, with subsequent asset behaviour in each regime. It is descriptive, shows sample sizes, and is not used as a trading signal.
